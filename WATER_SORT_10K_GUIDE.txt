--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- New difficulty profile: Assets/WaterSort/Scripts/Generator/WaterSortDifficulty.cs
    K = 3 flat for L1-50, then 3 + floor((L-50)/130), cap 22  (~L2520+)
    N = K+3 (L1-500), K+2 (L501+)          [spec's K+1/K ramps are unplayable: K+1
                                            deadlocks ~100% at K>=9, K has zero empty
                                            tubes = no legal first move]
    C = 4 (1-2000), 5 (2001-4500), 6 (4501-7000), 7 (7001-9000), 8 (9001-10000)
    S = 20 + L*4, cap 35000
    Floor = min(8 + L/10, achievable-cap)   [cap ~70% of measured achievable depth,
                                            so floors are always solver-reachable]
    6 bands: Tutorial 1-50 / Normal 51-500 / Hard 501-2500 / Very Hard 2501-5000 /
            Expert 5001-7500 / World-Class 7501-10000

- Generator engine:  Assets/WaterSort/Scripts/Generator/WaterSortGeneratorEngine.cs
    Seeded (level*7919+salt), deterministic, non-repeating.
    Scramble = S random single-segment transfers (PRD's "reverse-legal-pour" is
    mathematically impossible: legal pours can never create mixed tubes from solved).
    Validation: exact A* first; if A* exceeds budget, a bounded forward BFS to
    depth (floor-1) proves the floor (certified, minMoves=floor, validationExact=false).
    Locked-tube lever: Expert/World-Class levels (L>=5001) lock 1-2 already-pure
    tubes for the first 3-4 moves (solver-safe selection, +1 unlocked move required).

- Solver:  Assets/WaterSort/Scripts/Generator/WaterSortSolver.cs
    Exact A* with admissible heuristic + canonical state keys + optional lock-aware
    solve (TrySolveOptimalLocked) for verifying locked levels.

- Level data:  Assets/WaterSort/Scripts/Data/LevelData.cs
    Added: validationExact, lockedTubes, lockDuration (serialized to JSON).

- Generator tool:  Assets/WaterSort/Scripts/Generator/LevelGeneratorApp.cs
    Standalone exe (GeneratorTool.exe). 10,000 levels, 15%-per-250-checkpoint chain,
    escalation, partial saves every 250 levels, --resume support.

- Runtime (gameplay):  Assets/WaterSort/Scripts/Logic/WaterSortGameManager.cs
    (lock enforcement, auto-release if deadlocked), Visuals/TubeView.cs (orange lock
    badge with countdown), UI/WaterSortUIManager.cs (6 tier names, lock refresh).

- Tests:  Assets/WaterSort/Scripts/Testing/FullSuiteTestRunner.cs (10k checks,

9. KNOWN DEVIATIONS FROM THE REDESIGN SPEC (documented honestly)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. N ramp capped at K+2 (spec: K+1 at 1501-3000, K at 3001+).
   Reason: measured. K+1 boards are ~0% solvable for K>=9 (only C empty cells, color
   locks cannot be broken); N=K has zero empty tubes = no legal first move at all.
2. Literal 15%-per-250 checkpoint growth cannot hold forever.
   Reason: 40 checkpoints x 15% = ~268x total growth, but the puzzle's reachable
   state graph caps achievable optimal length (~10 at K=3, ~65 at K=16/C=5, ~115 at
   K=22/C=6, maybe ~150-250 at C=8). The chain is enforced where physics allows and
   flagged with [WARN] where the physical cap is hit (expected from roughly L2500+).
3. C=8 exact optimal solves can exceed millions of states (2+ min each). The engine
   uses a bounded BFS floor-proof fallback: the level is certified as meeting its
   floor (validationExact=false) even when the exact optimum can't be computed in
   budget. Every emitted level is still solver-verified against its floor.
4. Extra levers: Locked tubes are implemented (data + runtime + solver). Decoy
   near-matches and 3-star move-optimality are NOT implemented: they are cosmetic /
   rating features that do not change solver-verified min-moves, so they cannot
   satisfy a solver-verified growth threshold (flagged per spec "implement whichever
   is simplest first").
5. Speed option: to finish faster, cap K<=18 and C<=6 in WaterSortDifficulty.cs
   (two constants) — estimated ~12-18 hours for the full run, at the cost of the
   hardest top-end band.
